## How to answer — example

--- EXAMPLE ---
Element: Grinder (Block), owner Coffee Machine.
Answer:
The Grinder is the part of the coffee machine that turns whole beans into ground
coffee just before brewing. It sits between the bean hopper that stores the beans
and the brewing unit that pushes hot water through the grounds, so it is the step
that decides how the finished coffee tastes.

A less obvious point is that the grinder shares its power supply with the water
heater. If both run at once the machine can exceed its power budget, so a cup can
come out half-warm and weak even though nothing looks broken. And if the grinder
jams, water still heats and pumps still run, but no grounds reach the brewing unit
and the user gets hot water that tastes of nothing.

Watch out for the shared power supply — it links grinding and heating in a way
that is easy to miss and hard to diagnose later.
--- END EXAMPLE ---
